# Using the App

Every action documented in these guides — creating a vault, configuring categories and tiers, setting vesting schedules, minting passes, and claiming tokens — is available directly through the frontend. No technical knowledge, no code, no command line required. If you can connect a wallet, you can use Onchain Tokenomist.

**For project teams** — the app walks through the complete vault creation flow. Define your categories, configure your tiers, set your vesting schedules, and encode your entire token economy on-chain in a single guided transaction. What you submit is what goes on-chain. What goes on-chain cannot be changed.

**For admins** — pending proposals are visible and actionable directly from the app. Review the proposed recipient set, verify the configuration, and approve or reject — all from the interface without interacting with the contract directly.

**For token recipients** — connect your wallet to see every pass you hold, your current vesting status, how much is available to claim right now, and when your next unlock occurs. Claim directly from the interface in a single transaction.

***

The contract functions documented throughout these guides are what the app executes under the hood. They are provided for transparency and for developers who want to interact with Onchain Tokenomist directly. For everyone else, the app is the interface — the protocol is the guarantee behind it.
